5 On-Page SEO Mistakes That Are Killing Your Rankings

Published Sep 2026   |   4 min read

Many website owners spend heavily on backlinks while ignoring basic on-page fundamentals. Here are five mistakes I see most often when auditing client sites.

1. Missing or Duplicate Title Tags

Every page needs a unique, keyword-focused title tag under 60 characters. Duplicate titles confuse search engines and hurt click-through rates.

2. Thin Content

Pages under 300 words rarely rank for competitive terms. Focus on covering a topic thoroughly rather than padding with filler.

3. Slow Page Speed

Compress images, use lazy loading, and minimize scripts. A one-second delay in load time can significantly reduce conversions.

4. No Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links help search engines understand site structure and pass authority between pages. Link related content naturally within your text.

5. Ignoring Mobile Experience

With most traffic coming from mobile devices, a responsive, fast-loading mobile layout is no longer optional — it's essential for rankings.

Link Building

Why Quality Beats Quantity in Backlink Building

Published Sep 2026   |   3 min read

A common mistake businesses make is chasing backlink volume instead of relevance and authority. Search engines have become far better at detecting unnatural link patterns.

Focus On Relevance

A single link from a site in your niche carries more weight than dozens of links from unrelated, low-quality directories.

Check Trust Metrics

Domain rating, organic traffic, and spam score all matter. A backlink from a site with real visitors is far more valuable than one from an inactive blog.

Diversify Anchor Text

Over-optimized anchor text is a red flag for search engines. Mix branded, generic, and keyword anchors naturally across your link profile.

Sustainable link building takes patience, but it protects your site long-term and builds genuine authority.

How to Rank Higher on Google Maps in 2026

Published Sep 2026   |   5 min read

Local SEO has changed significantly, and Google Business Profile optimization remains the biggest lever for local visibility.

Complete Every Profile Field

Category, hours, services, and photos all impact ranking. An incomplete profile signals lower relevance to Google's algorithm.

Collect Consistent Reviews

Review count, recency, and response rate all factor into local pack rankings. Encourage happy customers to leave detailed reviews regularly.

Build Local Citations

Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) information across directories strengthens trust signals for local search algorithms.

Create Location-Specific Content

Pages targeting specific service areas help capture "near me" searches and long-tail local keywords.
